About the event

Open knowledge. Open collaboration.

Open source software lets people view, use, improve, and share code under an open-source licence. This event helps participants understand the process and take a practical first step.

Bring your curiosity, your laptop, and a willingness to learn. You can contribute code, documentation, testing, design ideas, or helpful bug reports.

What you’ll take away

  • Understand Git and GitHub basics
  • Learn issues, branches, commits, and pull requests
  • Collaborate respectfully with a project community
  • Find a contribution that matches your skill level
